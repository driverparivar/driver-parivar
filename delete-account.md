**Account Deletion Policy**

You may delete your account through the Driver Parivar app by navigating to the Settings menu and selecting the "Delete My Account" option.

Please note that once your account is deleted, all associated data — including job history, verified badges, ratings, and app preferences — will be permanently erased. This process is irreversible.

After deletion, you will no longer be able to access any paid features or restore previous settings linked to your account.

You may also cancel any active subscriptions via your Apple App Store or Google Play Store account.
